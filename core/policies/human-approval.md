# Human Approval Policy

## Required approvals

Human approves requirements before design.

Human approves architecture before implementation.

Human approves scope changes.

Human approves permanent decisions.

Human approves release or activation when required by the task.

## Gate behavior

Present the result, important evidence, risks, open decisions, and next action.

Offer approve, revise, or pause.

Wait for explicit approval.

Silence is not approval.

A tool result is not approval.

A specialist recommendation is not approval.

## Missing approval mechanism

If an adapter cannot collect explicit approval, pause the workflow.

Do not infer approval from a runtime default.

Do not replace Human approval with an agent self-check.
